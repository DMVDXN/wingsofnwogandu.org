Programs

Four pillars, one purpose

Our work is organized around four areas that shape a young person's future: education, health, opportunity and community.

Each pillar below describes an area of focus for the foundation. Specific initiatives will be listed under their pillar as they launch.

Education

Education opens the first door. This pillar is about making sure young people have what they need to learn and to keep learning.

  • Scholarships and educational assistance
  • School supplies and learning resources
  • Support for educational access and achievement
  • Scholarship opportunities Future initiative Learn more

Health

Young people thrive when they are well. This pillar is about health knowledge, wellness and access to care.

  • Health education and awareness
  • Wellness initiatives
  • Healthcare access partnerships
  • Youth-focused health resources

Opportunity

Talent is everywhere; opportunity is not. This pillar is about connecting young people with guidance, exposure and the chance to lead.

  • Mentorship opportunities
  • Career exposure and exploration
  • Leadership development
  • Resources that help young people develop their potential

Community

Children grow within families and neighborhoods. This pillar is about showing up for the communities that raise them.

  • Food and essential-resource initiatives
  • Holiday giving
  • Community outreach
  • Activities supporting children and families

Want to support one of these areas?

Tell us which pillar matters most to you, and we will be in touch about ways to help.
