The meaning behind our name

Legacy, life and the wings to rise

Nwoga

Nwoga represents the family legacy and the symbolism of the hawk: vision, strength, resilience and the ability to rise above.

Ndu

Ndu means life. It reflects the lives the foundation hopes to impact.

Wings

The wings represent the support and opportunity we hope to give the next generation.

Our mission

Wings of Nwoga Ndu empowers underserved youth by expanding access to education, health resources, mentorship, and opportunities that help them build brighter futures.

Our vision

A future where every young person has the resources, support, and opportunity to pursue their potential and create lasting change within their communities.
