Who we are

Opportunity changes what a young person believes is possible

Wings of Nwoga Ndu Foundation is committed to expanding opportunities for underserved youth. We believe the opportunities we receive can become opportunities we create for others, and that with the right resources and support, every young person can pursue their potential.
