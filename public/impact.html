Our Impact

Measured by lives, not headlines

Impact overview

How we think about change

Meaningful change shows up in the lives of young people and the communities around them. We look at whom our work reaches, what resources it puts in their hands, and what becomes possible for them as a result.

We publish only what we can verify and document. Numbers, stories and photographs appear on this page once they are confirmed and, where people are involved, once we have their permission.
