# Wings of Nwoga Ndu Foundation: website design brief

This is the source of truth for building the site. It records the foundation's brief as supplied on 2026-10-11, plus the build notes and open questions that follow from it. Where something is a proposal rather than part of the brief, it is marked **Proposed**.

## Foundation

| | |
|---|---|
| Legal name | Wings of Nwoga Ndu Foundation, Inc. |
| Short name | Wings of Nwoga Ndu |
| Domain | wingsofnwogandu.org |
| Tagline | Giving Every Child the Wings to Dream. |
| Hosting | GoDaddy (product type not yet confirmed) |

Write the name as "Nwoga Ndu", two words. Only the domain runs them together.

**Mission:** Wings of Nwoga Ndu empowers underserved youth by expanding access to education, health resources, mentorship, and opportunities that help them build brighter futures.

**Vision:** A future where every young person has the resources, support, and opportunity to pursue their potential and create lasting change within their communities.

**Website goal:** Establish a professional, trustworthy online presence for the foundation, communicate its mission, showcase its programs and impact, and encourage visitors to support or partner with the organization.

## Content rules

These apply to every page and override any layout convenience.

- Never show placeholder statistics as real results. Publish only figures that can be verified and documented, and hide any statistic that is not yet available.
- Do not present a program as active until it has launched or is ready to accept participants. Clearly separate existing programs from planned ones.
- Do not claim donations are tax deductible unless the foundation's status and the donation's eligibility support it.
- Protect children's privacy. Get consent before publishing identifiable information or images, and publish testimonials only with permission.
- Name partners and sponsors only with their permission.
- On Scholarships, show no deadlines, award amounts, eligibility criteria or application forms until those details are final.

## Site map

Navigation order: Home, About, Programs, Our Impact, Get Involved, Contact, then Scholarships (future). The navigation bar also carries the logo and a prominent "Get Involved" button.

### 1. Home

Purpose: introduce the foundation immediately, communicate its mission, and guide visitors toward taking action.

1. **Navigation bar:** logo, the page links above, and the "Get Involved" button.
2. **Hero:** headline "Giving Every Child the Wings to Dream." with a short introduction to the foundation and whom it serves. Primary button "Explore Our Programs", secondary button "Get Involved". A strong image reflecting youth empowerment, education, opportunity or community.
3. **Introduction:** brief overview of Wings of Nwoga Ndu and its commitment to expanding opportunities for underserved youth. Links to About.
4. **Our four pillars:** Education, Health, Opportunity, Community. Each has a short description, an icon or image, and a link to Programs.
5. **Our vision:** a short statement about helping young people build brighter futures and stronger communities.
6. **Impact preview:** verified accomplishments, initiatives and community contributions, added as they become available.
7. **Get involved call to action:** invite visitors to volunteer, partner, donate (once donation systems are ready) or contact the team.
8. **Footer:** logo and tagline, quick links, official contact email, social media links, copyright notice, Privacy Policy and other legal links.

### 2. About

Purpose: tell the foundation's story, explain its purpose, and establish trust.

1. **Our Story:** how the foundation came to be, the belief that the opportunities we receive can become opportunities we create for others, and the founder's motivation.
2. **The Meaning Behind Our Name:**
   - "Nwoga" represents the family legacy and the hawk symbolism associated with vision, strength, resilience and the ability to rise above.
   - "Ndu" means life and reflects the lives the foundation hopes to impact.
   - The wings represent support and opportunity for the next generation.
3. **Our Mission:** the official mission statement.
4. **Our Vision:** the official vision statement.
5. **Our Values:** (proposed website groupings based on the mission and brand story)
   - Opportunity and empowerment
   - Education and lifelong learning
   - Health and well-being
   - Community and service
   - Legacy and responsibility
6. **Founder's Message:** a personal introduction covering the motivation behind the foundation and the future it hopes to build. Professional photo if desired.
7. **Our Commitment:** meaningful, community-centered impact and expanding access to resources for underserved youth.

### 3. Programs

Purpose: explain the foundation's areas of work and the kinds of initiatives it supports. One section or card per pillar, each with its own image or icon and a short description of its purpose.

| Pillar | Covers |
|---|---|
| Education | Scholarships and educational assistance; school supplies and learning resources; support for educational access and achievement; future scholarship opportunities |
| Health | Health education and awareness; wellness initiatives; healthcare access partnerships; youth-focused health resources |
| Opportunity | Mentorship opportunities; career exposure and exploration; leadership development; resources that help young people develop their potential |
| Community | Food and essential-resource initiatives; holiday giving; community outreach; activities supporting children and families |

Add links to projects and initiatives as they launch. Each item needs a visible status so existing and planned work are never confused.

### 4. Our Impact

Purpose: demonstrate what the foundation has accomplished and how its work benefits communities.

1. **Impact Overview:** how the foundation measures meaningful change.
2. **Impact at a Glance:** young people reached, educational resources or supplies distributed, community initiatives completed, volunteers and partners engaged. Each figure appears only once verified; the section hides entirely if none are available.
3. **Our Initiatives:** completed projects, community activities and outreach, with descriptions, photographs, dates and outcomes where available.
4. **Stories of Impact:** testimonials and experiences, with permission.
5. **Our Growing Impact:** the long-term commitment to education, health, mentorship and community development.
6. **Partnership Recognition:** participating organizations or sponsors, with permission.

### 5. Get Involved

Purpose: give visitors clear ways to support the mission.

1. **Volunteer:** how individuals can contribute time and skills. Volunteer interest form or inquiry button when ready.
2. **Partner With Us:** invites schools, healthcare organizations, businesses, community groups and other nonprofits. Includes a partnership inquiry form.
3. **Support Our Mission:** donation information once the foundation is ready to receive donations. Online donations must use a secure payment platform and explain how contributions are used.
4. **Support an Initiative:** lets visitors express interest in education, health, opportunity or community projects.
5. **Spread the Word:** social media links and options to share the mission.
6. **General Interest Form:** name, email, organization (if applicable), area of interest, message.

### 6. Contact

Purpose: make it easy for people and organizations to reach the foundation.

- Short welcome message inviting inquiries
- Official foundation email address
- Contact form: full name, email address, organization (optional), subject or inquiry type, message
- Social media links
- Expected response time, once established
- Optional mailing address or other official contact information

Form requirements: submissions go to an actively monitored inbox, with spam protection and basic validation, a confirmation message after a successful submission, and a privacy notice explaining how submitted information is handled.

### 7. Scholarships (future page)

Status: not yet launched. The page can be prepared now but must stay clearly marked as a future initiative and accept no applications until the program is formally established.

Display for now:

> Scholarship program launching in a future program year.

Optional secondary message:

> We look forward to sharing more information about upcoming scholarship opportunities. Please check back for future announcements.

Optional button "Receive Scholarship Updates", only if an email sign-up system exists and is ready to manage subscriptions.

Structure once the program is ready: introduction; purpose and intended beneficiaries; eligibility requirements; application process; application timeline; award details; frequently asked questions; official scholarship contact information. Eligibility, process, timeline and award details read "to be announced" until then.

## Brand

**Personality:** hopeful, empowering, intelligent, warm, aspirational, culturally grounded, and committed to meaningful impact.

**Visual direction:** elegant, modern, warm and mission-driven. Use the hawk and wings symbolism where appropriate. Use authentic, respectful imagery of youth, education, community and opportunity. Keep colors, typography, spacing and button styles consistent across pages. Avoid crowded layouts and excessive animation.

### Colors

The brief names the colors but gives no values. Forest green and gold are sampled from the supplied logo; ivory, burgundy and charcoal are **Proposed** until the foundation supplies official values.

| Color | Hex | Source | Use |
|---|---|---|---|
| Forest green | `#1D4134` | Logo | Primary brand color: header, primary buttons, dark sections |
| Ivory | `#FAF6EC` | Proposed | Page background |
| Burgundy | `#6E1F2E` | Proposed | Secondary accent: secondary buttons, links, highlights |
| Gold | `#BE9A59` | Logo | Decorative accent: rules, icons, borders |
| Charcoal | `#2B2B2B` | Proposed | Body text |

Gold does not have enough contrast for text on ivory. Use it for decoration, or for large headings on forest green or charcoal only.

### Typography

| Role | Font | Use |
|---|---|---|
| Primary | Cormorant Garamond | Headings, hero headline, pull quotes |
| Secondary | Montserrat | Body text, navigation, buttons, forms |

Both are available from Google Fonts. Cormorant Garamond is light and fine, so set it at 600 weight or heavier and never use it for small text.

| File | Version | Use |

The full logo carries the line "Empowering the next generation to soar.", which differs from the tagline in the brief, "Giving Every Child the Wings to Dream." Confirm with the foundation which one is current.

## Functional and technical requirements

- Responsive design for desktop, tablet and mobile
- Accessible navigation, readable text, sufficient color contrast, image alt text and keyboard navigation
- Fast page loading and optimized images
- Search-engine-friendly page titles and descriptions
- Secure HTTPS connection
- Working contact forms with spam protection
- Privacy Policy and Terms of Use where appropriate
- Cookie notice if required by the technologies used and applicable laws
- Social media integration
- Basic website analytics with privacy safeguards
- Simple content management, so the foundation can update programs, impact stories and announcements without rebuilding the site
- Secure backups and a maintenance plan

## Phases

**Phase 1 (launch):** Home, About, Programs, Our Impact, Get Involved, Contact, and the Scholarships page in its "launching in a future program year" state.

**Phase 2 (as the foundation grows):** detailed project pages, scholarship applications, volunteer registration, online donations, impact reports, newsletter sign-up, partner and sponsor recognition.

## Build notes

- **Unverified content stays out of the markup.** Impact figures, initiatives, stories and partners should be driven by data, so an empty list hides its section instead of showing a placeholder.
- **Program status is a field.** Each program or initiative carries a status of active or planned, and the page labels planned items visibly.
- **Donations are off at launch.** "Support Our Mission" shows information only until a payment platform is chosen.
- **The hosting product decides the stack.** The content management requirement and the working forms both depend on which GoDaddy product is in use, so confirm that before choosing a framework.
